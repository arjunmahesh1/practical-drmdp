# Five-Dimensional Evidence Profiles

November 14, 2026. Uses the supplied manuscript's new deployment-shifts section,
not its old Results section. L1/L2/L3 are independent scopes per dimension, not
a common cumulative ladder. See PROTOCOL.md for exact definitions and pairing.

- index.json: 43 catalog cohorts, 297 fixed-setting/named-reference comparisons.
- <study>.json: every nominal, one-sided shift and complete-suite component test.
- comparisons.csv: both paired and independent intervals and eligibility flags.
- Paper recommendations are delivered separately in chat, not website tabs.
- videos.json: exact-cohort illustrative clips, including the three completed
  corrected Drawer SAC recordings; no cohort-success inference from a video.
- assets/profiles/: five new paper figures in vector PDF and PNG.

All source scientific curve/policy/outcome JSONs and historical figures are
unchanged. Only new derived profiles and a three-entry recording-overlay
addition were made. Incomplete runs remain unavailable; unverified axes cannot
establish suite scope. Ratios with nonpositive nominal references are unavailable.

The intervals are exploratory and pointwise, not multiplicity-adjusted. A broad
metric profile is not a certificate of activation or physical task success.
The old combined audit and descriptive summaries remain historical links.
