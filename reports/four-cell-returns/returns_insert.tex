Best-per-axis is descriptive ex-post full-grid selection, not held-out inference or physical-success evidence. No new rollout videos.
Lines show means and bands show $\pm1$ sample standard deviation across thirty policy-level episode means. The standard curve is ordinary TD3, fixed on every axis.
